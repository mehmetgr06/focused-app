Frequently Asked Questions

The Pomodoro technique breaks work into 25-minute focused sessions, followed by a 5-minute break. After 4 sessions, you take a longer break. In Focused, you can fully customize all durations to fit your personal rhythm.
Deep Focus Mode uses iOS Screen Time to block distracting apps during your focus sessions. You can select specific apps, categories (social media, games, entertainment), or block all at once. To use it, you'll need to grant Screen Time permissions.
Study Rooms are virtual spaces where you and others can focus together in real-time. You can create a public room (anyone can join) or a private room (invite only). A shared timer keeps everyone synchronized. Sign in is required to use this feature.
A Habit Chain helps you build consistency by checking off a habit every day without "breaking the chain." You can create habits with custom icons and colors, track your streaks, and view a heatmap of your progress over months.
No! The core features (Pomodoro timer, tasks, habit chains, weekly schedule) work without an account. Sign-in is required for Study Rooms and cloud sync across devices.
Focused automatically follows your device language. If your device is set to Turkish, the app will display in Turkish. For English, set your device language to English. You can also manually change the language in the app's Profile → Settings section.
