How it works

Getting started takes less than a minute. Just focus.

1

Set your session

Choose your work duration, pick a background, and enable Deep Focus to block distractions. Everything is customizable to your style.

2

Work deeply

Start your Pomodoro timer and enter deep work mode. Join a study room to focus alongside others or go solo — it's up to you.

3

Track your habits

After your session, check off your habit chains and update your task list. Watch your streaks grow and consistency compound over time.

4

Review your progress

Check your weekly stats, celebrate milestones, and plan your next session. Small improvements every day lead to extraordinary results.
