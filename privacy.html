Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At Focused, we take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Locally Stored Data: Most of your data, including your tasks, habit chains, and pomodoro history, is stored locally on your device by default.

Account Information: If you choose to create an account to use online features (like Study Rooms) or to sync your data, we collect your email address and basic profile information (like your display name).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide and improve the Focused app experience:

  • To sync your data across your devices (if you are signed in).
  • To allow you to participate in online Study Rooms.
  • To provide customer support when requested.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as Firebase for authentication and database services) to facilitate our app. These services have access to your Personal Information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

4. Screen Time and Deep Focus

Our "Deep Focus" feature uses iOS Screen Time APIs to help you block distracting apps. We do not transmit, collect, or store information about which apps you use or block outside of your device. This data remains strictly on your device.

5. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@focusedapp.io.
